## Three Different Models for "Free AI"

In a world where companies are racing to impose monthly subscriptions, platforms are emerging that try to break this rule in different ways. Some offer completely free services, others rely on advertising, and some offer a limited free service with a cheap paid plan. Three platforms are currently drawing attention: **AIBuilder**, **VibeFree**, and **8B**. Each has a different philosophy, a different business model, and a different level of reliability.

🔗 **AIBuilder:** [getaibuild.com](https://getaibuild.com)
🔗 **VibeFree:** [vibefree.dev](https://vibefree.dev)
🔗 **8B:** [8b.com](https://8b.com)

## AIBuilder: Unlimited App Building... on Paper

### What It Offers
AIBuilder is an AI app-building platform that markets itself as **100% free and unlimited forever**, with no credit card, no trial period, and no paywall. The idea: you write a description of what you want, the platform builds a complete interface in seconds, and then you can modify it with another sentence. The platform uses Anthropic models (Claude) and describes itself as a "philanthropic mission" similar to Lovable.

### Business Model
The claims are strong: "no message caps, no project caps, no feature gates." Everything is free for everyone, with no paid plans.

### Reality and Warnings
Here the picture becomes concerning. Reliability analyses indicate that the site is **very new** (created in May 2026, just months before this article was written), its traffic is **very low**, and it received a **high-risk score** from WebSafely, which advises against entering passwords, payment data, or personal information. There are no popularity signals or independent reviews supporting the "free forever" claims. A business model that offers an expensive service (generating complete apps via LLM models) completely free, with no ads, no data, and no paid plan, raises logical red flags.

## VibeFree: Free Chat... Paid for by Ads

### What It Offers
VibeFree is a free AI chat platform, **supported by advertising**. The site announces "388 million tokens" provided for free, and offers chat with multiple models, with additional features such as **cross-conversation memory**, **in-chat code generation (Artifacts)**, and **MCP** (Model Context Protocol) support for connecting external tools. There is also an **extension for VS Code** (and Cursor and Windsurf) that provides a free coding agent inside the editor, without needing an API key.

### Business Model: Ads Pay the Bill
The idea is clear and transparently disclosed: instead of a monthly subscription, **short ad cards** appear alongside replies. Advertisers pay upfront for these placements, and this money covers inference, servers, and bandwidth costs. When no ad is available, **VibeBucks** balance (which can be earned through tasks or offers) is used to cover the cost of the reply. You can also use the chat in the code editor, with control over ads (hide, report, or disable personalization).

### Assessment
VibeFree offers a practical and sustainable model: free service in exchange for your attention (ads). The transparency in explaining the funding mechanism, the editor extension, and the features provided (memory, MCP, Artifacts) make it a serious option for anyone who wants a free assistant without a financial commitment.

## 8B: Website Generation with One Sentence

### What It Offers
8B (not related to model size) is an **AI website builder**. You write a description of your business (restaurant, clinic, accountant), and the engine generates a complete design (colors, fonts, images, text) in a single HTML page. You can modify it through chat, then download the final HTML file or publish it.

### Technical Features
- **MCP Server:** Can be added to ChatGPT (in developer mode) to build websites directly from the chat.
- **Design Generation, Not Template Selection:** Unlike ready-made templates, 8B generates the design from scratch for each project.
- **HTML Export:** You can take the file and host it yourself.

### Pricing
The free plan lets you **design the entire website** and preview it, but **publishing** (putting it online) requires a paid plan. The paid plan starts at **$9/month** (billed annually), or $3.90 according to another pricing page. The platform also supports website generation via MCP for free (without an account) if you use ChatGPT.

### Assessment
8B offers a real and specific service (generating a landing page or small website), with a clear business model: free to design, paid to publish. This makes it a practical option for small business owners who want a simple website quickly, without committing to a long-term hosting platform (because they own the HTML file).

## Quick Comparison

| Platform | Service | Business Model | Reliability |
|----------|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **AIBuilder** | AI app building | "Free forever" (unconfirmed) | **Low** (new site, low traffic, security warnings) |
| **VibeFree** | Chat and coding assistant | Ad-supported | **Medium to Good** (transparent model, editor extension, clear features) |
| **8B** | Website generation | Free to design, paid to publish | **Good** (specific service, HTML export, MCP server) |

## Summary

"Free" AI is not a single concept. **AIBuilder** makes big promises but lacks sufficient evidence of sustainability or reliability. **VibeFree** offers a practical and sustainable solution through advertising, with transparency in funding and real features. **8B** offers a specific service (website generation) with a clear business model: free to design, paid to publish.

If you are looking for a free chat or coding assistant, VibeFree is the most logical choice. If you want a simple website quickly, 8B is the right tool. As for AIBuilder, until proven otherwise, treat it with extreme caution.
